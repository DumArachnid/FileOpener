---
name: Account reminders and feature grants
description: Product rules for optional account prompts and administrator-controlled rewards.
---

Account creation is optional. Recommend it no more than once per local calendar day, and stop reminding a user on a device after they sign in or create an account. Administrators may permanently unlock all built-in features for another selected account or grant that account points or shards.

**Why:** The user wants account creation encouraged without blocking workspace use or nagging existing account holders, while keeping feature unlocks and rewards under administrator control.

**How to apply:** Keep prompts dismissible and outside required workflows. Store administrator unlocks server-side so they persist across devices and future sign-ins.
